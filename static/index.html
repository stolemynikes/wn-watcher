timing

Reading your tabs costs Whatnot nothing, so this can be fast. "Confirm polls" is how many readings must agree before you get a push — 2 rides out a half-drawn page.

detection –

This finds giveaways by the words on the page, so it breaks if Whatnot rewords them. Edit a pattern and press try to see what it matches on the tabs open right now — then save. No file editing, no restart of anything but the watcher.
